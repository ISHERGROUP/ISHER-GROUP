Page 1 — Cell: The Unit of Life

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Main points

  • All living organisms are made of cells.
  • The cell membrane forms a selectively permeable boundary.
  • Cells may be prokaryotic or eukaryotic.

Page 2 — Cell Organelles

Nucleus: contains genetic material.

Mitochondria: important site of aerobic respiration.

Ribosomes: sites of protein synthesis.

Endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus: help in synthesis, processing and transport of cellular materials.
